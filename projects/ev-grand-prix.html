01 / CONTEXT

The project

I head the school’s Electric Vehicle Grand Prix effort, coordinating more than 80 students across seven teams while working directly on the vehicle. We built and raced the car in 2026. My technical contributions span wiring and repairs, telemetry, battery and motor research, CAD and 3D scanning, race preparation and troubleshooting. The project continues toward the 2027 season.

03 / ENGINEERING NOTES

Technical detail

A complete vehicle, not a single subsystem

The car brings together a 48 V powertrain, controls, sensing, mechanical packaging and the team processes needed to test safely. Each change has to work with the rest of the vehicle, under constraints of mass, energy, reliability and race rules.

My hands-on role

I wired and repaired electrical connections, investigated faults, worked on telemetry integration, supported CAD and body development, and prepared the car with the driver. Dedicated case studies explain the electrical system, telemetry, digital modelling and vehicle testing.

At the competition

At Karting Town, Sharjah on 14 February 2026, the team received four EVGP awards. At the separate 2026 SABIS STEAM competition hosted at SIS Yas Island, the car placed third overall and was highest ranked in the Unmodified category.
