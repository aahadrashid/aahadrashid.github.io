01 / CONTEXT

The project

Our team scanned the existing electric race car and turned the physical body into a 3D mesh. That original model became the starting point for an edited body shape intended to be more aerodynamic. I contributed to the scanning, mesh review and CAD work. The gallery shows both stages; the revised shape is a design iteration, not a measured drag result.

03 / ENGINEERING NOTES

Technical detail

From car to model

The workflow moved from the physical car to a 3D scan and mesh. The original model preserved the existing body shape and gave the team geometry to inspect before making changes.

Body-shape revision

We then edited the digital body to explore a more streamlined exterior while checking how the revised shape related to the original car. The side, front and three-quarter views document that design step.

Scope of evidence

The gallery separates the original scan from the revised body model. These screenshots document the design process; they do not by themselves establish a measured aerodynamic gain.
