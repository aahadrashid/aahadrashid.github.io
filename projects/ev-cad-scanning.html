01 / CONTEXT

The project

Our team used a 3D scan of the existing vehicle as a starting point for a digital representation of the car. I contributed to scanning, mesh and CAD work so physical geometry could inform component packaging and later body design.

  3. 03

03 / ENGINEERING NOTES

Technical detail

From car to model

The workflow moved from physical measurements and scan data to a mesh, then to more usable CAD geometry. Mesh cleanup and dimensional checks matter because rough scan data cannot directly answer packaging questions.

Design use

The model supports conversations about chassis space, batteries, motor, controller, wheels and body shape. It also gives the team a common geometry to review before making physical changes.

Scope of evidence

The gallery shows views of the scanned mesh. They document modelling work; they do not by themselves establish a measured performance gain.
