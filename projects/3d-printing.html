01 / CONTEXT

The project

I led a ten-student 3D printing and modelling team for an international STEAM competition. We moved from CAD models through slicing, printer setup, physical prototypes and testing. Workshop photographs and certificates from 2023 and 2024 document the activity.

What I did

  3. 03

03 / ENGINEERING NOTES

Technical detail

From model to part

The process connected digital geometry with a physical prototype. Each iteration required checking whether the printed part matched the intended form and function, then adjusting the model or print setup.
