01 / CONTEXT

The project

I led a seven-member team building a VEX V5 robot for the High Stakes challenge at an international STEAM competition. My contributions included drivetrain CAD, mechanical assembly, programming and debugging, driver testing and match strategy. Build photographs and robotics participation certificates document the work.

03 / ENGINEERING NOTES

Technical detail

From CAD to field test

We developed the drivetrain and mechanisms, assembled the robot, and tested it with rings and field elements. Testing showed how reliably the robot could perform the competition tasks and informed driver practice and match planning.

Team coordination

Leading the build meant dividing design, fabrication, code and testing work across seven students, then bringing those parts together into one competition robot.
