01 / CONTEXT

The project

Telemetry has been my main individual technical focus on the EV. I integrated and tested an earlier measurement setup on the car, tracing power, grounding, continuity, communication and signal-quality issues. I later finalized a revised architecture for planned use in 2027. The revised hardware was a design at the 2026 race.

03 / ENGINEERING NOTES

Technical detail

2026 vehicle integration

For the earlier system, I checked sensor power, grounding, wiring continuity and communication between components. I tested voltage, current, speed and location channels individually before checking them together on the car. Shielding and signal-wire routing helped address interference and inaccurate readings, including issues affecting the Cycle Analyst.

What the data is for

The measurement plan covers pack voltage, battery current, speed and vehicle position. Logged or transmitted readings could help the team compare tests, understand energy use and make better-informed race decisions.

Finalized 2027 design

The revised architecture specifies an ESP32-C3 for sensing and low-level communication, an ADS1115 ADC, a Raspberry Pi 4B for processing, a cellular/GNSS module, a voltage-divider circuit and Hall-effect current sensing. This design is finalized for planned use on the 2027 car.

Design iteration

The earlier ESP32-based approach was simpler. The revised design separates sensing, processing and connectivity. IMU and wheel-Hall concepts were removed after the measurement plan was narrowed to what GPS could provide.
